### Making a better fit easier to find.

Measurement Matcher compares garment measurements on TheRealReal with saved preferences, helping shoppers filter listings by measurements that matter to them.

A practical project connecting a real decision problem with structured information, configurable rules, and a usable tool.

**Methods:** JavaScript · Decision support · Data export

The extension supports garment-specific matching, independent measurement tolerances, and CSV exports of checked listings. Missing measurements are skipped rather than treated as known mismatches.
