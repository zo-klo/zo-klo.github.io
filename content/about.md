# Interdisciplinary Mixed-Methods Research | Product Evaluation and Design | Strategy | Musician

I am a social science researcher with a background in developmental psychology, survey design, and mixed-methods research, and I love thinking about the underpinnings of human behavior and decision-making. My work focuses on utilizing these skills to design, evaluate and improve products’ usability and functionality at every stage in the product lifecycle.

I hold a BS in Psychology from Haverford College, with minors in Global Asia Studies and Neuroscience, and an MA from Columbia University's Graduate School of Arts and Sciences in Quantitative Methods in the Social Sciences.

Outside of work, I am a classically trained singer, and I dabble in violin, ukulele, and piano. I love drawing and painting; my favorite mediums are acrylic paint and alcohol-based markers. I've been a huge fantasy nerd since childhood, and I love the ASOIAF series and strategy RPGs.
