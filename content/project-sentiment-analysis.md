### Finding patterns in what people say.

[View project on GitHub](https://github.com/zo-klo/STM-Affordability-Analysis)

A sentiment analysis project exploring synthetic survey responses through text cleaning, sentiment analysis, and structural topic modeling in R.

An exploration of how unstructured language can become a source of evidence. Uses synthetic data for methodological demonstration.

**Methods:** R · Sentiment analysis · Topic modeling

The workflow brings together tidytext, textclean, and the stm package to prepare survey text and examine its sentiment and themes.
